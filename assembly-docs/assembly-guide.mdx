## Purpose

This work instruction documents the current RADR wireless remote assembly process from prepared PCB and printed parts through final device test.

Use these instructions with the active PCB, printed parts, and BOM release for the build.

## Required Parts and Consumables

1. Gather the required parts before starting assembly.

2. Confirm the assembly set includes:

    - `RADR-PCB-assembly`
    - `screen`
    - `battery`
    - `front-housing`
    - `back-housing`
    - `battery-flap`
    - 2x `side-button`
    - `middle-button`
    - `power-slider-button`
    - `knob`
    - `weather-stripping`
    - 5x `BC-0408LTFB` tri-lobe screws
    - `hot-glue`

3. If all required parts are present, continue onward.

## Tools

- Rasp
- T8 bit for tri-lobe screws
- Electric screwdriver or hand driver
- Isopropyl alcohol
- Microfiber cloth
- Razor blade
- Hot glue gun
- Small screwdriver

## Prepare the PCBA

1. Deburr the edges of the `RADR-PCB-assembly` with a rasp until the board edge is smooth.

    <Photo caption="Deburr the PCBA edges until smooth." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/pcba-deburr-edges.webp" alt="RADR PCB assembly with edge areas marked for deburring" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

2. Wipe the marked weather-strip area on the PCBA with `isopropyl-alcohol` and a `microfiber-cloth`.

    <Photo caption="Clean the marked weather-strip area." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/pcba-clean-weather-strip-area.webp" alt="Marked weather-strip area on the RADR PCB assembly" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

3. Attach a 2.5 cm piece of `weather-stripping` to the center of the cleaned area.

    <Photo caption="Weather stripping installed on the PCBA." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/weather-strip-installed.webp" alt="Weather stripping installed on the RADR PCB assembly" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

4. Fit the printed `power-slider-button` over the PCBA on/off switch.

    <Photo caption="Power switch cover installed." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/power-switch-cover-installed.webp" alt="Printed power switch cover installed over the PCBA on off switch" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

5. Connect the 800 mAh LiPo `battery` to the PCBA.

    Keep the PCBA switched off after connecting the battery. The PCBA produces a green light when powered on and does not produce that light when switched off.

    <Photo caption="Battery connected to the PCBA." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/battery-connected-to-pcba.webp" alt="LiPo battery connected to the RADR PCB assembly" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

6. Confirm the PCBA is deburred, padded, fitted with the power switch cover, connected to the battery, and switched off before continuing.

## Prepare the Front Housing

1. Use a `razor-blade` to cut a small triangle off both bumpers on the `front-housing`.

    These cuts stop the bumpers from catching on the PCBA during installation.

      <Photo caption="Trim both bumper features." style={{ maxWidth: "360px", margin: "0" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/front-housing-bumper-cut-lines.webp" alt="Front housing bumper features marked for trimming" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>
      <Photo caption="Trimmed interior bumper reference." style={{ maxWidth: "360px", margin: "0" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/front-housing-bumper-trimmed.webp" alt="Front housing bumper after trimming" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>
    </PhotoGrid>

2. Remove the protective film from the `screen`.

    <Photo caption="Remove the display film." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/screen-protective-film-removal.webp" alt="Protective film being removed from the RADR display" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

3. Install the `screen` into the `front-housing`.

    Insert the screen into the bottom lip of the interior front housing first. Gently flex the housing outward while pressing the screen into the upper screen lip. Confirm the screen is flush against the housing.

    <PhotoGrid>
      <Photo caption="Insert the screen into the bottom lip first." style={{ maxWidth: "360px", margin: "0" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/screen-bottom-lip-insertion.webp" alt="Display being inserted into the lower lip of the RADR front housing" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />

      <img src="/radr/assets/screen-seated-flush.webp" alt="Display seated flush in the RADR front housing" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>
    </PhotoGrid>

4. Run a thin, low-profile bead of `hot-glue` along the screen edges.

    Keep the glue bead in contact with both the screen edge and the plastic housing. The housing plastic should melt slightly into the glue.

    <Photo caption="Low-profile glue bead around the screen edge." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/screen-hot-glue-bead.webp" alt="Hot glue bead around the RADR display edge" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />

5. Drop the printed buttons into the `front-housing`.

    <Photo caption="Buttons installed in the front housing." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/buttons-installed-in-front-housing.webp" alt="Printed RADR buttons installed in the front housing" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

6. Confirm the bumpers are trimmed, the screen is glued in place, and the buttons move freely before continuing.

## Install the PCBA

1. Feed the screen ribbon through the cutout in the PCBA, then press the PCBA flush into the `front-housing`.

    <Photo caption="PCBA seated in the front housing." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>

    </Photo>

2. Open the screen ribbon connector on the PCBA with a small screwdriver.

    <Photo caption="Open the screen connector." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/screen-connector-open.webp" alt="Open screen ribbon connector on the RADR PCB assembly" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

3. Insert the screen ribbon into the connector and close the connector.

    <Photo caption="Screen ribbon connected." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/screen-ribbon-connected.webp" alt="Screen ribbon inserted into the RADR PCB connector" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

4. Tuck the screen ribbon into the housing.

    <Photo caption="Screen ribbon tucked into the housing." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/screen-ribbon-tucked.webp" alt="Screen ribbon tucked into the RADR housing" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />

5. Confirm the PCBA sits flush, the ribbon connector is closed, and the ribbon is tucked away from pinch points before continuing.

7. Confirm the housing is fully closed, all screws are seated, and the battery cover is secured before continuing.

## Install Knob and Test

1. Use a small screwdriver to clean any excess filament from the interior knob circle.

    <Photo caption="Clean the knob bore before installation." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/knob-interior-cleaned.webp" alt="Interior bore of the RADR knob after cleaning excess filament" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

2. Align the encoder shape with the interior knob circle shape and press the `knob` onto the encoder until it softly clicks.

    <Photo caption="Press the knob onto the encoder." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/knob-installed-on-encoder.webp" alt="RADR knob being pressed onto the rotary encoder" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

3. Turn on the device and test as per protocol.

    <Photo caption="Device powered on for final test." style={{ maxWidth: "520px", margin: "0.75rem 0 1.5rem 1.5rem" }}>
      <img src="/radr/assets/device-test-on.webp" alt="RADR wireless remote powered on for final test" style={{ width: "100%", height: "auto" }} />
    </Photo>

## Final Assembly

1. Confirm the PCBA edges are smooth and do not catch on the housing.
2. Confirm the weather stripping is centered and adhered to the PCBA.
3. Confirm the power switch cover moves the on/off switch cleanly.
4. Confirm the front-housing bumpers are trimmed.
5. Confirm the screen is flush and glued with a low-profile bead.
6. Confirm the buttons are installed and move freely.
7. Confirm the PCBA sits flush in the front housing.
8. Confirm the screen ribbon is fully inserted and the connector is closed.
9. Confirm the screen ribbon and battery wires are routed away from pinch points.
10. Confirm the housings and battery cover are fastened with tri-lobe screws.
11. Confirm the knob is seated on the encoder.
12. Confirm the device powers on and passes the current functional test protocol.
